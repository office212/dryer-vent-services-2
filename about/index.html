Who We Are

Dryer Vent Services is a local, owner-operated business serving Staten Island, New York and New Jersey since 2020. Over 1,000 jobs later, we've built a reputation for thorough, honest work - and we intend to keep it that way.

We specialize exclusively in dryer vent systems: cleaning, repairs and full installations. Because this is all we do, we do it right.

Every job includes an airflow test before and after the cleaning - so you know the vent is actually clear, not just serviced on paper. We inspect the entire line from the back of the dryer to the exterior exit, and we don't call the job done until airflow confirms it.

Beyond cleaning, we repair damaged or non-compliant exhaust ducts and install new vent systems from scratch - including proper rigid metal ductwork, transitions and exterior hoods. If your setup needs more than a cleaning, we handle it completely.

  • Repairs & full installations - not just cleaning
  • Upfront, flat-rate pricing - no surprises
