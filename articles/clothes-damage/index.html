Is Your Dryer Ruining Your Expensive Clothes?

You invest good money in your wardrobe. But if you notice your clothes are shrinking, fading prematurely, or the elastic is breaking down faster than it should, your washing machine might not be the culprit.

The Overheating Trap

A dryer relies on a steady flow of air to regulate its internal temperature. When a vent is clogged with lint, that airflow is choked off. The dryer still produces heat, but with nowhere to go, the temperature inside the drum skyrockets well past safe levels for most fabrics.

Frying Your Fabrics

Natural fibers like cotton and wool will shrink drastically when exposed to excessive heat. Synthetic athletic wear and garments with elastic waistbands or spandex will actually begin to melt on a microscopic level. This causes the garments to lose their stretch, become brittle, and eventually tear. If your clothes come out of the cycle feeling uncomfortably hot to the touch, your vent is likely blocked.

Protect Your Investment

Replacing ruined clothes adds up quickly. Before you blame the clothing brand or buy a new appliance, have your dryer vent inspected. A clear vent ensures your machine runs at the correct temperature, extending the life of your favorite outfits.
