Does Your Pet Make Your Dryer a Fire Hazard?

We love our dogs and cats, but their shedding can cause serious problems for our home appliances. If you have furry friends at home, your dryer vent is at a significantly higher risk of catching fire.

The Pet Hair Problem

Pet hair behaves differently than standard cotton lint. It is often coated in natural oils from the animal's skin, making it sticky and highly flammable. When pet hair passes through the lint screen and enters the exhaust duct, it easily clumps together and sticks to the walls of the pipe.

This sticky base layer catches everything else that passes by, accelerating the creation of a massive, solid blockage that restricts airflow.

More Frequent Maintenance Required

Homes with multiple pets cannot rely on the standard "once a year" cleaning recommendation. Depending on the shedding volume, pet owners often need professional dryer vent cleanings every 6-8 months to maintain safe airflow and prevent overheating.
