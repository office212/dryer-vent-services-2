Is Your Pet Making Your Dryer a Ticking Time Bomb? What Every NY & NJ Pet Owner Must Know

We all love our furry family members, but if you own a cat or dog, you are ignoring one of the biggest safety and efficiency hazards in your home: your dryer vent. Pet fur and dander are the leading causes of dangerous, hidden dryer vent clogs.

According to the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA), failure to clean dryer vents is responsible for thousands of residential fires every year in the United States. While you might clean the lint screen after every load, pet hair and dander are notoriously difficult to capture and always find a way to bypass that screen, building up inside the exhaust duct.

The Hidden Dangers of Pet Hair

Unlike lint from your clothing, pet fur and dander are highly combustible. They act as a powerful fuel source, capable of igniting easily. This buildup restricts airflow, causing the machine to overheat and create an extremely dangerous situation behind your walls.

The risk of a catastrophic house fire is exponentially higher for pet owners. Every extra furry load of laundry is adding layers of highly flammable fuel to your dryer vent.

The Ultimate Solution: A Professional Clean
