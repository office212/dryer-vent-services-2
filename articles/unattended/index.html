The Fatal Mistake: Why You Should Never Run Your Dryer While Sleeping or Away

It is a common habit for busy families in Staten Island and New Jersey. You throw in a load of laundry before bed or right before heading out to work. But this simple convenience is one of the leading causes of devastating house fires.

The Speed of a Lint Fire

A dryer fire does not start slowly. When highly flammable lint inside your machine or vent ignites due to overheating, it spreads with incredible speed. Within seconds, the fire can move from the back of the dryer into the walls of your home. If you are awake and home, you might smell the smoke or hear an unusual noise in time to act. If you are sleeping or away, you lose those life-saving seconds.

No One to Hear the Warning Signs

Most dryers give off subtle warning signs before a fire starts - a burning smell, a loud thumping noise, or a sudden increase in heat. When the machine is running unattended, these signals go completely unnoticed. By the time a smoke detector wakes you up or a neighbor notices smoke coming from your house, the fire has often already caused irreversible damage.

The Clogged Vent Connection

While mechanical failures can happen, the vast majority of dryer fires are caused by a lack of maintenance. A clogged vent forces the dryer to run hotter and longer, creating the exact conditions needed for lint to ignite. Running a dryer with a dirty vent is dangerous - running it while you are not there to monitor it is a gamble with your home and family.
