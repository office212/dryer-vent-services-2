Is Your Dryer a Time Bomb? 5 Signs of a Clogged Vent

Dryer fires don't happen completely out of nowhere. The machine usually gives several warning signs that the vent is choking before a disaster occurs. Here are the 5 signals you must never ignore.

1. Clothes Take Multiple Cycles to Dry

If your clothes are still damp after a normal 45-minute cycle, the hot air cannot escape. This is the first and most common symptom of a severe blockage.

2. The Dryer is Burning Hot to the Touch

A functioning dryer should be warm, not hot. If the top of the machine or the clothes inside are dangerously hot to the touch, the internal thermostat is failing to regulate the trapped heat.

3. A Burning Smell

If you smell something burning, turn the dryer off immediately. This is the smell of lint inside the machine beginning to scorch from extreme back-pressure heat.

4. The Laundry Room is Unusually Humid

A proper vent sends moisture outside. If your laundry room feels like a sauna while the machine is running, the steam is leaking back into the room due to a blockage.

5. The Outside Flapper Doesn't Open

Go outside while the dryer is running. The flapper on your exterior vent should be blowing a steady stream of air. If it's barely moving, the air is trapped inside your walls.
