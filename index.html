Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about dryer vent maintenance.

Why is dryer vent cleaning important?
Cleaning your dryer vent prevents lint buildup, which is the #1 cause of dryer fires. It also helps your clothes dry faster and lowers your energy bills.
How often should I clean my dryer vent?
Experts recommend cleaning your dryer vent at least once a year. If you have a large family or use the dryer frequently, you may need it every 6 months.
How do I know if my vent is clogged?
Signs include clothes taking longer to dry, the dryer getting very hot, or a burning smell. If you notice any of these, call us immediately.
