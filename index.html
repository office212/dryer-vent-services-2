FAQ

Common Questions

Why is vent cleaning important?
It prevents lint buildup (the #1 cause of dryer fires), dries clothes faster, and lowers energy bills.
How often should I clean my vent?
Experts recommend cleaning it at least once a year. Larger families may need it more often.
