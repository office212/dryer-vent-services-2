Safety Upgrade

Specialized Bird & Pest Guards

Keep unwanted wildlife out of your home without compromising your dryer's safety. We install advanced guards designed specifically to block pests while allowing dangerous lint to safely escape.

The Danger of Standard Screens

Many homeowners make the mistake of putting a regular wire mesh or screen over their dryer vent to stop birds. While this stops the birds, it immediately traps all the lint. Within weeks, the vent becomes completely blocked, creating a severe fire hazard and breaking your dryer.

  • The Right Balance: Our guards feature spacing wide enough for thick lint to blow out, but tight enough to keep birds, mice, and squirrels from getting in.
  • Durable Materials: Made from high-quality, weather-resistant materials that withstand harsh winters and hot dryer exhaust.
  • Code Compliant: Unlike DIY screens, our covers meet fire safety regulations for proper appliance ventilation.
  • Permanent Solution: Stop paying for repeated nest removals by securing the entry point correctly the first time.

Our Installation Process

We ensure your new guard is securely mounted and the vent is fully clear before we finish the job.

1. Exterior Assessment

We examine the current vent hood on the side of your house or roof to determine the correct size and mounting style for your new guard.

2. Clearance Check

Before putting the guard on, we verify there are no existing nests or debris blocking the line just inside the wall.

3. Secure Mounting

We install the heavy-duty guard over the exhaust flap, ensuring it is firmly anchored to the siding or brick so animals cannot pry it off.
