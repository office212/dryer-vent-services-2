Frequently Asked Questions

How often should dryer vents be cleaned?
Most homes need dryer vent cleaning every 1 to 2 years. Heavy use, long vents or bends may require annual cleaning.
Do you serve beyond Staten Island?
Yes. We serve Staten Island first and the greater New York and New Jersey area.
Can clogged vents cause dryer fires?
Yes. Lint buildup is highly flammable. Professional cleaning restores airflow and reduces risk.
