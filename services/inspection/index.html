Data-Driven Safety Checks

A quick visual check isn't enough. Many severe blockages and disconnected pipes occur deep within your walls or floorboards. By measuring air velocity and pressure, we can pinpoint issues before they become dangerous.

  • Accurate Diagnostics: We measure CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute) to determine exactly how well your vent is exhausting.
  • Hidden Blockages: Identify crushed ductwork or heavy lint accumulation behind walls.
  • Appliance Health: Ensure your dryer isn't straining against back-pressure, which causes heating elements to burn out.
  • Peace of Mind: Real data that proves your home is safe from dryer fire hazards.

How We Test Your System

We believe in transparency. We test your airflow before and after our services so you can actually see the difference.

1. Baseline Reading

Using a digital anemometer, we take an initial measurement at the exterior vent hood to determine the current level of restriction.

2. Visual & Structural Audit

We inspect the transition hose, the wall connections, and the overall routing to identify code violations or damaged materials.

3. Post-Service Verification

After any cleaning or repair work, we take a final measurement to prove to you that maximum airflow has been restored.
