New Dryer Vent Installation

Whether you're building a new home, renovating, or moving your laundry room, we provide expert installations that are built to last and meet all modern safety codes.

Built for Safety and Performance

A poorly installed vent is a ticking time bomb. We design systems with the shortest possible route and the fewest turns to ensure your dryer breathes easy and operates at peak efficiency from day one.

  • Code Compliance: We follow all local building codes for vent diameter, material, and length.
  • Rigid Metal Ducting: We use only heavy-duty rigid metal for the smoothest interior surface and maximum fire safety.
  • Expert Routing: Minimized bends mean faster drying times and less lint accumulation over time.
  • Professional Exterior Finishing: High-quality hoods and wall caps that look great and protect against weather and pests.

The Installation Process

From the first measurement to the final seal, we take pride in every detail of your new ventilation system.

1. System Design

We plan the most efficient route from your dryer to the exterior wall, ensuring minimal resistance and maximum airflow.

2. Precision Fitting

We install rigid metal ductwork with all joints pointed in the direction of airflow and securely sealed with aluminum foil tape.

3. Exterior Termination

We install a high-performance exterior vent hood that opens fully for exhaust but seals tightly against birds, pests, and backdrafts.
