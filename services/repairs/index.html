Core Service

Dryer Vent Repair & Replacement

Damaged, crushed, or disconnected dryer vents are a major fire hazard and ruin your dryer's efficiency. We repair, replace, and reroute your ductwork to meet strict safety codes.

Signs You Need Vent Repair

If your dryer vent is made of flimsy plastic, ribbed foil, or is crushed behind the machine, your home is at risk. We replace these dangerous setups with proper, high-quality materials.

  • Crushed Hoses: Restricted airflow from machines pushed too far back.
  • Unsafe Materials: We replace illegal vinyl and thin foil with rigid metal ducting.
  • Disconnected Joints: Leaking lint and moisture directly into your walls or laundry room.
  • Water Damage: Condensation buildup inside poorly routed or sagging ducts.

Our Repair Process

We don't just patch things up. We rebuild your system to ensure maximum airflow and total safety compliance.

1. Inspection & Diagnosis

We identify leaks, breaks, and non-compliant materials behind your walls, in the attic, or behind the machine.

2. Duct Replacement

We replace dangerous flexible ducts with safe, rigid or semi-rigid heavy-gauge metal ducting that won't sag or crush.

3. Sealing & Securing

We use professional aluminum foil tape (never regular duct tape) to seal all joints securely, preventing lint buildup and air leaks.
