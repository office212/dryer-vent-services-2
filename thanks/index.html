A Few Things That Help

A little preparation makes the visit faster and smoother.

Clear the path: make sure we can reach the back of the dryer and move it safely if needed.

Know your vent exit: if you know where the vent leaves the house, point it out when we arrive. If not, we will find it.

Keep pets comfortable: the dryer will be moved and the vent run tested, so a quiet room for pets helps everyone.
